# External strategy scope

Only the frozen three-component Ensemble, Common210 FullHistory and Top5_NoLoss13w are included in the external strategy result. Separate final-year annualised-return tables and charts are omitted. Standalone external Ranker models, predictions, simulations and comparisons have been removed.

V1 demonstrates preparation and fitting of the three Ensemble components only. It has no external prediction or backtest stage and no second Ensemble performance report.

The delivery retains predictions, weekly capital and verification records. Per-stock selection lists and order/holding ledgers are not exported; selection and accounting are independently checked in memory. Runs execute from start to finish without checkpoint recovery.

Reproduction starts from the supplied processed weekly data. Raw data acquisition and reconstruction are outside this delivery's scope.
